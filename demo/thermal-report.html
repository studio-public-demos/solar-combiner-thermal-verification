Enclosure Heat Transfer — FRP Wall Correction

IEC TR 60890 is calibrated for sheet-steel enclosures (reference U ≈ 5.5 W/m²·K). FRP has a much lower thermal conductivity, so the wall adds thermal resistance and the internal temperature rise is higher for the same loss.

ParameterValue
Wall thickness t3.5 mm
FRP thermal conductivity k (polyester + 25 % glass)0.25 W/(m·K) (range 0.20–0.35)
Wall conduction resistance R = t/k0.014 m²·K/W
Sheet-steel reference U5.50 W/(m²·K)
FRP effective U = 1/(1/5.5 + 0.014)5.11 W/(m²·K)
Correction factor = Usteel/UFRP1.077 (1.06–1.10)
FRP is outside the official IEC TR 60890 scope (sheet-steel). The correction above is a first-order engineering adjustment. A conservative enclosure-industry rule-of-thumb for polyester (U ≈ 3.5 W/m²·K) would give a factor of 1.57 → ΔT top ≈ 24.5 K (internal top ≈ 74.5 °C). Confirm with the enclosure manufacturer's published thermal data.

  • Against the IEC 61439-1 typical 50 K permissible rise (85 °C-class components), the 16.8 K top rise leaves ~33 K margin → enclosure is thermally adequate by calculation.
  • At 50 °C ambient with 85 °C-class devices, usable internal rise is 35 K; top rise 16.8 K leaves 18.2 K margin → still passes.
  • Component hot-spots (screening) range ≈ 67–94 °C; fuse estimate (~110 °C) is model-limited and resolves to ~73–78 °C using published gPV dissipation data.
  • FRP wall raises the rise by ~7 % over sheet-steel; a conservative polyester U ≈ 3.5 W/m²·K bound gives ΔT top ≈ 24.5 K (internal top ≈ 74.5 °C), still within limits.
  • Additional outdoor consideration: an IP66 FRP box in a solar farm will also absorb direct solar irradiation (adds several watts to tens of watts) — not included here; add it to the loss total for a worst-case check.

Limitations

  • Temperature-rise verification by calculation (IEC TR 60890, referenced by IEC 61439-1); not a type-test certificate.
  • Table-driven factors (surface b, k/c curves, exponent) follow the published IEC method structure and must be reconciled against the licensed standard text.
  • FRP is outside the official 60890 scope; the 1.077 material correction is a first-order engineering adjustment based on U-steel = 5.5 W/(m²·K) and k-FRP = 0.25 W/(m·K).
  • Component temperature rises are first-order P/(h·A) screening estimates with assumed cooling areas; certified values require manufacturer device thermal data.
  • Solar irradiation on the enclosure and spare-string/SPD/joint losses are not included in the 64.6 W total.
